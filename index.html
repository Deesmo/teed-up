How pricing works

You set your own number. The club's guest fee is fixed — that goes to the pro shop. Everything on top of it is yours, and it's yours to name. A seat at a course nobody can get on is worth what someone will pay for it.

Weekends and majors weeks price differently. Flag the date as high-demand and it's marked on the sheet. Saturday mornings at a top-100 club clear at three to five times a Tuesday afternoon.

You're paid when the round is played, not when the seat is claimed. Guest cancels inside 24 hours, you keep half.

The member is still responsible for the guest. Your name is on the register. Price accordingly, and decline anyone you wouldn't put in your own group.
